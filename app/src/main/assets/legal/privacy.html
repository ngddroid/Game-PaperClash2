1Who we are

Paper Clash 2 (“the game”) is made by NG Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains what information the game uses, where it is kept and what choices you have. It applies to the Paper Clash 2 app for Android.

Under data protection laws such as the GDPR, NG Studio is the “controller” of any personal information covered by this policy. In plain words: we are the ones responsible for it.

Email: [contact email]

Playing the game is also covered by our Terms of Use.

2What data the game handles

To work, the game saves a small amount of game data on your device:

  • Progress

    The levels you have unlocked, your stars, quests, achievements, sticker album and simple play stats.

  • Coins and items

    Your coins, boosters, plane skins and ribbons, and what you have equipped.

  • Settings

    Sound, music, vibration, graphics and control choices, and whether you have finished the tutorial.

  • Nickname

    The player name you type. It is only shown inside the game on your device. It doesn’t need to be your real name.

  • Daily rewards

    When you last claimed a daily gift or free spin, so the game knows when the next one is ready.

We use this data only to run the game: to save your progress, remember your settings and show your nickname on screen. It stays on your device. We can’t see it, and the game itself never sends it anywhere. Android’s own backup feature is explained in section 4.

If you email us

If you choose to contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and help you. We never use it for marketing, and we delete it once we no longer need it.

3What we do not collect

Paper Clash 2 does not collect, store or share:

  • your name, email address, phone number or home address (your nickname never leaves your device);
  • your location;
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone;
  • your advertising ID or any other device identifier;
  • analytics, usage tracking or crash reports;
  • payment details (there is nothing to buy);
  • account or login details (there are no accounts).

We don’t sell, rent or trade any data, we don’t build profiles and we don’t use your information for advertising.

4Storage and backups

Your game data lives in one save file, plus a spare copy so that a crash while saving can’t wipe your progress. Both are kept in the game’s private storage on your device. Android keeps this storage private to the game, so other apps can’t read it.

We don’t run servers for the game, and we don’t keep a copy of your data anywhere.

Android backups

If backup is turned on for your device, Android’s Auto Backup may include the game’s save file in your device backup (for example, in your Google account). Android may also copy it when you move to a new phone. This is how your progress can come back after you reinstall the game.

These backups are made and stored by Android and Google, not by NG Studio, and Google’s privacy policy applies to them. We can’t see or access them. You can turn backup on or off in your device settings.

5Permissions

Vibration VIBRATE

Lets the game give small haptic buzzes, for example when you claim land or pick up a power-up. It gives no access to your data. You can switch it off any time in Settings → Vibration.

That is the only permission the game uses. It does not ask for access to:

  • Internet
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Photos and files
  • Notifications

6Children

Paper Clash 2 is made for a general audience, and its content is friendly for players of all ages.

We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age that applies where you live). The game never asks for personal information, and the nickname stays on the device.

If you are a parent or guardian and think your child has sent us personal information, for example by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

7Your choices and deleting data

  • Sound, music and vibration: turn each one on or off in Settings.
  • Nickname: change it whenever you like in Settings.
  • Delete everything: go to Settings → Reset progress. This erases all of the game’s saved data on this device. It can’t be undone.
  • Other ways: clear the game’s storage in your device settings (usually Settings → Apps → Paper Clash 2 → Storage → Clear storage), or uninstall the game.
  • Backups: a copy in your Android backup is managed by Android and Google. You can manage or delete backups, or turn backup off, in your device or Google account settings.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, or to object to how it is used. Because the game keeps your data on your device, you can do most of this yourself with the options above. For anything else, including emails you have sent us, contact us and we will help. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8Security

Keeping things simple also keeps them safe. Your game data stays in the game’s private storage on your device, and the game sends nothing over the internet. There is no server of ours holding your data and nothing to intercept on the way.

No system is perfectly secure, though. Anyone who can unlock your device may be able to open the game and see your progress, so we recommend using a screen lock.

9Third parties and future advertising

Today

The game contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code from any other company, and it shares no data with anyone.

  • Google Play and Android. You get the game from Google Play and it runs on Android, including Android’s backup feature. Google’s own terms and privacy policy apply to those services. Google may give us general statistics, such as install numbers, and crash reports from people who chose to share diagnostics with Google. These don’t tell us who you are.
  • Other apps you open. Buttons such as Share or Rate us, and email links, open another app on your device, such as Google Play, your email app or an app you pick to share with. Those apps have their own privacy policies.

Ads in the future

We may add advertising in a future version of the game, for example rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Ad services can collect information such as your device’s advertising ID. Before any ads appear, we will:

  • update this policy to explain what is collected, by whom and why;
  • ask for your consent where the law requires it;
  • update the Data safety section on the game’s Google Play page.

Ads can only arrive with a game update. The version you are using now has no ads and no code from any ad company.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add a feature or when the law changes. The latest version is always here in the game, with its “Effective” date at the top.

If we make an important change, such as adding ads, we will tell you in the game before it applies and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
